# Code of Conduct

ClipForge welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels.
Our goal is a respectful, useful environment for collaboration.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and communicate respectfully.
- Give constructive, specific feedback; discuss the code or idea rather than
  attacking the person.
- Welcome questions and help newcomers participate.
- Respect different viewpoints, privacy, and personal boundaries.
- Accept reasonable moderation and move disagreements toward a solution.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, personal attacks, unwanted sexual
attention, disclosure of private information, and deliberate disruption are
not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other
official project spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove inappropriate content,
close disruptive threads, or restrict participation when necessary. Responses
should be proportionate to the behavior and its impact.

Report concerns through GitHub's reporting tools or a maintainer's published
contact method. Do not post another person's private information in a public
report. Maintainers should handle reports discreetly and consider the needs of
those affected.
